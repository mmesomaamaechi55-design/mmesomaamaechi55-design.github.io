ABOUT

A voice between literature, education and real life.

Amaechi Mmesomachukwu GodLiveth is a Nigerian writer, educator and literary scholar with interests in creative writing, English and literary studies, teaching and conversations around young people's lives.

His work brings together the imaginative freedom of storytelling and the discipline of academic inquiry — creating space for stories that entertain, ideas that teach and conversations that matter.
