ABOUT ME

Creating. Learning. Helping. Growing.

I am Amaechi Mmesomachukwu GodLiveth — a writer, educator, speaker, mental wellness advocate and creative with a deep interest in people, stories, ideas and the experiences that shape our lives.

I studied English and Literary Studies at Nwafor Orizu College of Education, Nsugbe, Anambra State. My academic journey strengthened my love for language, literature, education and creative expression.

As a writer, I work across different forms including fiction, articles, poetry and ghostwriting. I enjoy using words to tell stories, communicate ideas and help people express things that can sometimes be difficult to put into words.

I am naturally curious and open to opportunities. I enjoy learning, exploring new fields and taking on experiences that allow me to grow, contribute and discover new possibilities.

MENTAL WELLNESS & HUMAN CONNECTION

Helping people navigate life with more understanding.

I am deeply passionate about mental wellness, emotional well-being and human connection. I care about helping people navigate life's difficulties, relationship challenges, self-worth, personal struggles and the many experiences that can leave people feeling overwhelmed or misunderstood.

My interest in this area comes from a genuine desire to see people do well. I believe that sometimes people need a safe, compassionate conversation, encouragement and someone willing to listen without immediately judging them.

FAITH

Faith is part of my story.

I am a Christian and a lover of God. My years at The King's Ambassadors Seminary School, Atani, Anambra State played an important role in shaping my outlook on life, faith, service and purpose.

My faith continues to influence the way I see people, purpose, growth and the responsibility we have to make a positive difference wherever we find ourselves.
