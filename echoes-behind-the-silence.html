Echoes Behind the Silence

By Mmesomachukwu GodLiveth Amaechi
The people have spoken from lecture halls wide, From crowded hostels where weary dreams hide, They speak through their struggles, their laughter, their pain, Yet their voices fall softly like unwelcomed rain. A student sits quietly at the back of the class, Watching another long semester pass, The future seems distant, the burden seems near, But nobody notices the weight of the fear. Friends gather together beneath evening skies, Sharing their victories, disguising their sighs, For not every smile tells the whole story true, Some hearts are breaking while appearing brand new. The nation keeps moving through hardship and strain, Young people keep hoping through sunshine and rain, They speak of tomorrow, of chances untaken, Yet often their confidence slowly is shaken. They ask for good learning, for jobs and for peace, For doors of opportunity finally released, But answers arrive with uncomfortable delay, As dreams keep drifting farther away. Still voices survive in discussions at night, In poems and stories that challenge the fight, In friendships that strengthen the weak and the tired, In visions of futures not yet fully acquired. For every muted microphone waiting somewhere, There are hearts still courageous enough to care, There are hands still reaching, refusing to part, And hope still survives in the human heart. So let us keep speaking though silence may grow, For change often starts where small voices flow, The people have spoken; let all barriers fall, And let every voice finally matter to all.
